Songs by Heart

Songs by Heart brings live, interactive music to people living with dementia in memory care communities. An Opera Saratoga singer leads each visit, inviting residents to join in through familiar songs.

Bring Songs by Heart to Your Community What a Visit Looks Like ↓

Making music for, and with, people living with dementia.

What a Visit
Looks Like

A professional Opera Saratoga singer leads a live sing-along with the residents, choosing and pacing the songs by following cues from the people in the room.

Everyone takes part in their own way. Some residents sing along. Some clap or move to the music. Some talk about what a song brings to mind, and some simply listen.

The singer invites people in one at a time: mirroring a resident’s movements, offering a spoken or gestured prompt, making eye contact, and holding hands when it’s welcome.

Between songs, conversation grows out of the music itself, and the songs become something the room shares.

Ways to join in

  • Sing
  • Clap
  • Move
  • Talk
  • Listen
