Festival Artist Program

Saratoga Springs, NY

Spend the summer making opera in Saratoga Springs. Festival Artists perform on the mainstage, work alongside experienced colleagues, and develop their craft through rehearsals, coachings, and performances.

What You’ll Do

The mainstage season is the heart of the program. Every Festival Artist has a part in it, and each assignment is built around the artist: their skills, their repertoire and where they are in their development.

Beyond the mainstage, assignments may include cover work, concerts, master classes and community engagements. Each track below separates what you’ll do from opportunities that vary by assignment.

Singers

You’ll

What you’ll learn

Career Development

The second-oldest singer training program in the country.

Built into the residency

  • Industry auditionsEach summer, Festival Artists can audition for visiting company leaders, artist managers, conductors and directors.
  • Guest master classesRecent guests have included Broadway conductor Laura Bergquist, artist manager Ana de Archuleta, Metropolitan Opera baritone Michael Chioldi, Metropolitan Opera French diction coach Bénédicte Jourdois, former Laffont Competition Associate Director Brady Walsh, and Chicago Opera Theater music director Lidiya Yankovskaya.
  • Practical career skillsFinancial planning seminars, and a partnership with OPERA America’s career development program.
  • After the summerAlumni are regularly invited back as principal guest artists and recommended to other organizations.

Residency at a Glance

What to expect for time, pay, housing and getting around.

Timing and availability

  • The 2027 program runs within a May–June 2027 window. Exact residency dates for 2027 have not been published yet.
  • Plan to be available for the whole residency. Releases are generally not granted because of the schedule.

Stipend

  • The residency is paid, with a weekly stipend. 2027 rates will be confirmed with the next published program details.

Housing and transportation

  • Shared housing in the Saratoga Springs area is provided for participants only, not for spouses or partners, children, other relatives or pets.
  • Housing is several miles from rehearsal and performance spaces, and public transportation doesn’t reach the venues. Plan to bring a car or make other arrangements.
  • Opera Saratoga provides a transportation stipend, a weekly gas stipend for drivers, and transportation options for artists without a car.

Eligibility

  • For undergraduate students, graduate students and emerging professionals.
  • There is no age limit. Participating artists are generally in their 20s to early 30s.
  • International applicants must show they are eligible to work in the U.S. and confirm this before applying. Opera Saratoga does not obtain the paperwork.
