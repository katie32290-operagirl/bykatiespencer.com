Your First Opera

Never been? Good. Here’s what to expect.

What should I wear?–

Whatever you’re comfortable in. You’ll see everything from jeans to cocktail attire. Many people like to dress up a little for a night out, but there’s no dress code.

When should I arrive?–

At Universal Preservation Hall, seating opens about half an hour before the performance, and the venue asks you to arrive no later than 15–20 minutes before the start. Leave extra time to park.

How do supertitles work?–

When a production is sung in another language, an English translation of the words is displayed in time with the singing, so you can follow along. Your event page says whether a performance has supertitles.

How long is it, and is there an intermission?–

It depends on the production. Runtime, intermission, language and supertitles are listed on each event page. La Traviata, for example, runs 85 minutes with no intermission.

What if I’m running late?–

Late seating depends on the venue and the production. Check your event page or call the venue’s box office before you go.

Accessibility & Ticket Help

Universal Preservation Hall

  • Wheelchair-accessible seating and restrooms on the main level and in the balcony. Ask for accessible seating when you buy tickets.
  • Drop-off on Washington Street, on the right side as you face the building. The atrium entrance is about 60 feet from the street on a paved sidewalk.
  • A hearing loop is available.
  • Patrons who are blind or partially blind can contact the box office for appropriate seating.
  • Accessible parking spaces in the Woodlawn Garage, Congress Plaza and Putnam Street lots.
