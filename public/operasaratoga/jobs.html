Festival Artist Program

2027 Festival
Artist Program

2027 applications are closed.

A paid summer residency in Saratoga Springs for singers, pianist/coaches, conducting fellows and directing fellows. Festival Artists are part of every mainstage production and work alongside the company’s artists and staff.

Assignments, training and career development are on the full Festival Artist Program page.

Get Opera Saratoga Updates View 2027 Application Details on YAP Tracker ↗

Four tracks

  • SingersPerform principal, supporting and comprimario roles, cover roles and sing chorus in the mainstage season, plus concerts, coachings and master classes.
  • Pianist/CoachesPlay and coach rehearsals for the season’s productions, working alongside the music staff.
  • Conducting FellowsAssist the conductor in rehearsals and performances as part of each production’s artistic team.
  • Directing FellowsAssist the stage director in rehearsals as part of each production’s artistic team.

Program at a Glance

Program window
May–June 2027
Application fee
None for the 2027 cycle
Pay
Paid residency with a weekly stipend
Housing
Shared housing in the Saratoga Springs area, for participants only

A car or other transportation is needed; travel and gas stipends are provided
Work eligibility
You must be eligible to work in the U.S.

What to Prepare

2027 Application Requirements. Requirements may change for future cycles, and the complete submission process is on YAP Tracker.

Every applicant

  • ☐One-page résumé (PDF)
  • ☐High-resolution headshot
  • ☐Previous Opera Saratoga or other young artist program participation, if any

Singers

  • Two contrasting opera aria videos, recorded within the last 6 months
  • One aria must be by Mozart
  • One Golden Age musical theater excerpt (1940s–1960s)
  • Contemporary repertoire is encouraged

Auditions were by video. Selected candidates received further instructions.

Pianist/Coaches

  • Two recordings of operatic repertoire, made within the last 12 months
  • Video or MP3 accepted

The Head of Music Staff contacts candidates about next steps.

Conducting Fellows

  • A video of you conducting (operatic repertoire preferred)
  • A video or MP3 of your accompanying skills, made within the last 12 months
  • Piano skills are preferred

The Head of Music Staff contacts candidates about next steps.

Directing Fellows

  • No video or audio in the initial application

Candidates invited to interview were asked to bring a portfolio.
