What Hosting Involves

Exact dates can vary by guest. If you’re not sure your availability fits, ask us. We’re happy to talk it through.

  • ✓A private bedroom for each guest
  • ✓Kitchen access and Wi-Fi
  • ✓Access to laundry, if available
  • ✓A smoke-free household
  • ✓A home within a 30-minute drive of Saratoga Springs

Hosts aren’t expected to provide meals or transportation.

The Host Experience

Local households make it possible for Opera Saratoga to bring dozens of professional artists and technicians to Saratoga Springs each summer. Hosts get to know a young performer or production staff member early in their career, and a behind-the-scenes view of the festival.

Many past hosts have formed lifelong friendships with their guests.

For host households

  • An invitation for your whole household to attend a dress rehearsal
  • Recognition in Opera Saratoga’s seasonal program
  • Invitations to the Festival Artist Welcome, the end-of-season party and other company gatherings
