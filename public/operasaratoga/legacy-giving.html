Legacy Giving

For the audiences and artists still to come.

Ways to Leave a Legacy

Most common

Bequests

A bequest is a gift made through your will or living trust. With your advisor, you can decide whether to leave a specific amount, a percentage of your estate, or a portion of what remains after other gifts are made.

You may also direct your gift to a particular program or purpose. Please talk with Opera Saratoga about your intentions so we can make sure your wishes can be carried out.

Sample bequest wording
“I devise and bequeath to Lake George Opera Festival Association, Inc., dba Opera Saratoga, Saratoga Springs, New York, [dollar amount of gift, or the word ‘all’, or a percentage of the estate] to be used for its general purposes.”
Share this with your attorney, who can adapt it to your plans.

Retirement Plans

Ask your plan administrator about naming Opera Saratoga as a full or partial beneficiary of an eligible account, such as an IRA, 401(k) or 403(b). They can provide the forms you need.

Life Insurance

Ask your insurance company about naming Opera Saratoga as a full or partial beneficiary of your policy. They can provide a change-of-beneficiary form.

Charitable Remainder Unitrusts

A charitable remainder unitrust can provide payments during your lifetime, with the remainder passing to Opera Saratoga. It’s a more involved arrangement, so discuss whether it fits your goals with your estate or financial advisor.

Every situation is different. Please review your plans with your legal or financial advisor to choose the right kind of gift and meet the legal requirements for it.
