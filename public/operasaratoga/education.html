The Magic Flute Education Tour

Bring live opera to your students. Professional artists perform Mozart’s tale of magical instruments, a true love story and kindness triumphing over cruelty, right in your school, then stay to answer students’ questions.

At a glance

For memory care communities

Songs by Heart

Professional singers lead interactive sing-alongs in memory care communities, making music for and with people living with dementia or Alzheimer’s.

Inquire About Songs by Heart

Or email info@operasaratoga.org

How a visit works

  • Singers use therapeutic techniques such as mirroring, verbal and gestural prompts, direct eye contact and holding hands with residents.
  • Residents are encouraged to join in singing, clapping and dancing.
  • Conversations grow out of the songs themselves.
